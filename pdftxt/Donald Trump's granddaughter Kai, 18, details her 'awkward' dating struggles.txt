President Donald Trump's granddaughter Kai Trump is opening up
about her dating life - and what it's really like to have Secret Service
following your every move. 
On Wednesday, 18-year-old Kai made an appearance on the
Impaulsive podcast with Logan Paul, where she spoke about a wide
range of topics, including her Grandpa's presidency and her golfing
career.
Kai is the eldest child of Donald Trump Jr., and his ex-wife, mom
Vanessa Trump.
She has been a prominent feature in Trump's presidency, as she
made a moving speech at the 2024 Republican National Convention,
and has been vlogging the behind-the-scenes content of her life on
YouTube, including clips from his inauguration last January.
Now, she's sharing what it's really like to be the granddaughter of a
U.S. President, as she admitted that dating can be 'awkward.'
'What is dating like for you?' Paul asked the soon-to-be University of
Miami student.
